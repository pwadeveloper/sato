# Review, 20 November 2026: old design, new content

**Current. Wins where it conflicts with anything earlier on presentation.**

Two builds were in front of the client: `main` (the design he has been
reviewing since September) and the `NewDesign` branch (the 10 November
design refresh, which also carried the 16 November oil and gas
integration). His call:

- **Keep the old design's overall look.**
- **Take the new design's content updates.**
- **Headings were too bold** ("Welcome to Sato Engineering", "About Sato
  Engineering"); the new design's lighter headings on mobile are the
  reference.
- **Infrastructure and Oil & Gas must link to each other.** Moving between
  them meant going back to Home.
- **Both holdings sit under Services and under Projects.**

## What was done (branch `old-design-content-update`)

### Content from NewDesign

Every content commit on `NewDesign` after the shared base was carried over;
the three design commits (the refresh, the second pass, the phone-menu fix)
were not.

- The two Water photo fixes of 10 November, and the last two World Bank
  visit photographs on Sagamu intake.
- The two service groups, Infrastructure and Energy, and the two-column
  mega-menus with Oil & Gas's anchors as sub-items. These are what put both
  holdings under Services and Projects.
- The Oil & Gas service page in deck order, the `/projects/oil-gas` proof
  page, use cases, the About / Clients / Contact consortium changes, the
  Canada office, OG cards, and the decision record.

Components written for the new design (`StatStrip`, `FlowDiagram`,
`CycleDiagram`, `ResultsGrid`, `ServiceOverview`, the HTML value map,
`UseCaseCard`, `UseCaseLayout`, `HomePillars`) run here in this design's
type: Archivo throughout, no serif, no motion. A small compatibility layer
lets them run unedited where possible: `Reveal` is a plain wrapper,
`.eyebrow` is this design's uppercase label, `paper` is white, and
`font-mono` resolves to Archivo.

**Home** keeps its layout (copy beside the photograph, then the motto) and
gains the new content: the full name over "Engineering, infrastructure and
upstream energy", the Infrastructure and Oil & Gas buttons, the two
pillars, the labelled figures, the featured use case and the closing call
to action. The new design's marquee, project rail and survey band are not
carried.

### Lighter headings

`--text-display`, `--text-h1`, `--text-h2` and `--text-h3` are weight 600
(were 900, 900, 800, 700). Sizes are unchanged.

### Holding switch

`components/HoldingSwitch.tsx`, a strip directly under the header on every
page in one of the two holdings: which one the reader is in, a button to
the other, and a link to the other's projects (or services). It opens:

- Infrastructure: `/services/infrastructure`, the four discipline pages,
  their four project categories and every project in them.
- Oil & Gas: `/services/oil-gas`, `/projects/oil-gas` and its use cases.

From a services page the button goes to the other holding's services; from
a projects page, to its projects. Infrastructure's projects are
`/projects#infrastructure` (the overview's rows now carry their group as an
`id`). Every word is in `site.holdings`. It is on the page, not fixed,
because service pages already have a sticky sub-nav.

Energy Services, Digital Twin and Research belong to neither holding and
carry no strip.
