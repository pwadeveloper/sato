read these pages.

**Summary (one line, used on cards and as the page's opening):**

> Turning asset data into a working digital model that operators can plan and maintain from.

**Capabilities — framing:**

> A digital twin is a model of a physical asset that is kept true to it: built from the asset's own drawings, instrumentation and operating records, and accurate enough that a change can be tested on the model before it is made on the plant. Used well, it turns a small number of measurement points into a picture of the whole system.

> Sato approaches this from the infrastructure side. We already supply, install and commission the metering and instrumentation such a model depends on, and we already hold the asset records — drawings, as-builts, inspection histories — that give it something to be built from. The modelling and simulation are delivered with our technical partners.

> This page describes a capability. Sato has no completed digital twin project to cite yet, and delivered work will be described here as it is finished.

**Asset data and information management**

- Capture and structuring of asset records: drawings, as-builts, inspection and maintenance histories
- Information management standards and workflows, so a model can be kept current rather than rebuilt
- Digitalisation of paper maintenance, inspection and commissioning records

**Instrumentation and telemetry**

- Field instrumentation, metering and telemetry specified and installed to feed an asset model
- Integration with existing metering, SCADA and utility billing data
- Condition and performance dashboards for the people operating the asset

**Modelling, simulation and virtual testing**

- Physics-based and data-driven models of structures, plant and process systems
- Virtual testing of operating changes, uprating and modifications before they are made
- Model validation against measured behaviour, and re-validation as the asset changes
- Reservoir, well and production digital twins for oil and gas assets

**Solutions:**

> What a maintained model is actually for. Each of these is a decision an operator has to make anyway; the twin changes what it is made on.

**Condition monitoring from a few sensors** — A model calibrated against measurement infers what is happening across a structure or a system from the handful of points where instrumenting it is practical. That is usually the difference between monitoring an asset and instrumenting all of it.

**Maintenance planned on evidence** — Inspection and intervention planned against what the measurements and the model say about condition, rather than against a fixed calendar that is either too early or too late.

**Testing a change before it is made** — Operating changes, modifications and uprating tried on the model first, where being wrong costs nothing and where the answer arrives before the shutdown is booked.

**Real-time production optimization** — For oil and gas assets, the real-time optimization, AI-assisted production and reservoir work set out under Oil & Gas Services, delivered with the same partner team. The solutions stack there — surveillance, analysis, optimization, transformation — is the same one applied to a producing field.

**Metering data and water accounting** — Bulk and consumer metering brought together so a water undertaking can see where water and revenue are being lost. Sato has supplied and installed metering at that scale; the analysis built on top of it is what is new.

**Partnerships:**

> Sato delivers digitalization and digital twin work with its technical partners. The modelling, simulation and data science sit with them; the asset knowledge, the instrumentation and the site delivery sit with us.

> It is the same technical partner team that delivers Sato's oil and gas services.
