## What you are approving, and what you are not

These drafts describe **what each service offers**. They are not claims that
Sato has already delivered that work. Where the text was drawn from the partner
reference sites, only the description of the capability was taken — no project,
client, certification, staff number, year, location, statistic or award of
theirs appears anywhere. Sato does not claim another company's track record.

The question for each service is simply: **can Sato deliver this, and do you
want it offered?** Strike anything you are not comfortable standing behind in
front of an oil company's vendor-verification team — that is exactly who will
read these pages.

Two of the six say in their own body text that Sato has no completed projects
in that area yet. That is deliberate and I would keep it: it is the sentence
that stops the rest of the page reading as a claim.

The production build is blocked until all six are approved.
