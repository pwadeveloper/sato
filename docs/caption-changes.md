# Caption, title and narrative changes

Every caption, project title and narrative the new site uses, next to the version it replaced. Generated by `node scripts/audit-site-content.mjs`.

Until 9 November the only changes allowed to an old-site caption were ALL CAPS to sentence case, clear typos, and taking out the country and the former company name. The client's caption brief of 9 November (`docs/decisions/plain-captions.md`) replaced that rule for photograph captions: Water Resources and Civil Engineering & Road captions describe what each photograph shows, with no place, client or institution name (two water-meter captions excepted); Building & Construction captions read “Work in progress” or “Completed”, and two keep the COLPLANT name. Alt text follows the captions. Project titles and narratives are unchanged by it.
